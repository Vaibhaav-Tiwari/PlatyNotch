Island: a notch that shows what it can do

The last round cut so hard that the product disappeared. Features hid behind a text box, half the panel sat empty, search didn't find its own features and presets changed nothing visible. This redesign puts every core tool one click away, uses all of the space, and brings colour from the things you care about (your music, your sky), aimed at people aged 18 to 30.

What's wrong with what you have now

Tools are invisibleTimer, Note, Remind, Clipboard, Shelf and Memory exist, but only if you know to type. Nobody types into a box they don't know about.
Search is dumb"games" and "notes" offer "Search memory" and "Remind me". A feature name should find the feature first.
Dead space"Nothing up next" takes half the panel. Every slot needs a useful fallback.
Grey everythingColour was banned except for state. To a 22-year-old it reads as a settings page, not a product.
Presets do nothing visibleEveryday, Developer and Minimal flip widget switches that the new Home ignores. Weather showed in one and not another.
⌥⌘K closes the notchFrom another app it should focus the search, not close. Fix already in progress.

Who this is for

Students, developers and creators aged 18 to 30. The apps they live in are Spotify, Apple Weather, Arc, Raycast, Notion, Discord and iOS itself. Those apps share four habits we should copy:

  • Content is the colour. Spotify tints the screen with the album art; Apple Weather paints the sky you're under. The interface stays dark and quiet so the content can glow.
  • Everything is one tap away. iOS Control Center shows a row of round buttons, not a menu. You can see what's there.
  • Big, friendly numbers. Temperatures, timers and percentages in a rounded face, large enough to read at a glance.
  • Personality in small doses. Platy is the mascot: present in the pill and empty states, never in the way.

The keyboard stays as the fast path for people who want it (⌥⌘K), but it's never the only path.

The new Home

Three zones, always filled: a Now Playing card, a Today card (weather plus your next thing), and a dock of round buttons for every tool. A tool opens inside the panel and the back arrow returns to Home.

Presets that mean something

A preset now changes what you see, and Settings shows a live preview before you apply it.

Everyday

Music, plans, quick capture.
Home: music and today. Dock: Timer, Note, Remind, Clipboard, Shelf, Games. No Agents tab. The pill shows music first.

Developer

Everyday plus your coding agents.
The Agents tab and usage rings are on. The dock swaps Games for Memory. The pill shows agents first, and file actions and window snapping are on.

Minimal

Invisible until it matters.
The notch stays hidden until something is live. Home: music and today only, with the dock folded into the search button.

You can still customise: in Settings › Dock, drag to reorder and pick up to 8 tools.

Visual system

  • Chrome is black, content brings colour. Album art glows behind its card. The weather card is the sky. State colours (green running, amber needs you, red error) stay reserved for state. This replaces the "colour only for state" rule, which made everything grey.
  • Cards: #121214 fill, 16 pt radius, 12 pt padding. Dock buttons are 38 pt circles in #1C1C1F with 17 pt icons and 10.5 pt labels.
  • Type: SF Pro for text (13 pt body, 14 pt titles), and SF Pro Rounded for big numbers (34 pt temperature, 54 pt timer).
  • Icons: SF Symbols. Weather uses the multicolour set; everything else is monochrome.
  • Motion: one spring for open, close and tool morphs. Art glow and weather motion run only while open.
  • Platy: in the header, the pill and empty states, and nowhere else.

Behaviour fixes already underway

  • Search ranking: tools first, then commands, then memory.
  • ⌥⌘K: from another app it takes focus and opens search. It closes the panel only when the panel already has focus and search is open.

Decisions for you

1Dock: the 8 tools in figure A. Should Focus get its own button, or stay a mode inside Timer?
2Colour comes from album art and weather. This deliberately relaxes the strict monochrome rule.
3Weather location comes from macOS Location, with a city name you can set if you'd rather not share it.
4Music empty state: suggestions come from the last-played app's recents where available, otherwise only "Resume".

Build order

  1. Search and ⌥⌘K (in progress).
  2. Home: the Now Playing and Today cards, the weather backgrounds and the empty states.
  3. The dock and in-panel tools: Timer, Note, Remind, Clipboard, Shelf, Memory, Games.
  4. Presets with preview, the Dock settings, the pill states and the Agents polish.

Each step merges to main on its own, after review and CI, so you can test as it lands.
